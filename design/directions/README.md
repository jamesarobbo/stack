# Three visual directions

Throwaway screens for a direction pick. Open `index.html` in a browser. James picked Kitchen, and the live app now uses that language. These three pages stay as the options that were compared.

Same facts on every screen: Alex's example stack, $175 a month, about $27 of duplicate spend, dose score 79 out of 100. Discover uses the listed stacks already in the library. Kept counts show only from 3.

Held in all three: sentence case, no emoji, no em or en dash, no house icon, no fake social proof, no countdown or urgency. A score under 80 is not drawn as a success.

## A. Journal

A light, editorial reading of the same product. High contrast serif for the number and the title, a quiet grotesque for the interface, and a deeper teal with gold reserved for money.

Bends: Inter is replaced by Newsreader and Outfit. The teal and amber are richer than the tokens. The canvas is a warm paper white, not the cool app gray. Corners are nearly sharp.

## B. Instrument

A light app with dark stages for the number. The audit is a chart: what the score lost, and where each dose sits against its upper limit.

Bends: dark hero surfaces. A glow behind the ring. Extra chart colours (gold for a dose near the limit, rose for a doubled ingredient) beyond teal. Amber money text sits on the dark slab, not on the amber wash. Inter is replaced by Schibsted Grotesk.

## C. Kitchen

A warmer, more human front door. Illustrated still lifes, a cream ground, sage for the action, and a clay accent in the drawings. This is the direction the live app now uses.

At the time of the pick it bent the old rules: a cream canvas, clay in the drawings, Nunito instead of Inter, and warm charcoal for a score under 80. Money stayed gold.

## Kitchen, clearer

`kitchen-v2/` holds three static palette trials (Daylight, Orchard, Linen). They are not wired to the app. The live tokens stay on the shipped Kitchen cream until a later pass.
