# Stack library

Discover lists shared stacks that the owner has opted in. A link-only share is the default. It stays out of Discover and the public page stays `noindex`. Listing and indexing use the same switch: List in Discover. The owner can unlist at any time. Unlisting removes the stack from Discover and from other people's Saved lists.

## Tabs

New is listed stacks by the day they were first shared, newest first. A stack that has never been edited is still listed. Editing does not hide it and does not move it up this tab.

Top ranks by how many different people copied the stack in the last 30 days. Older copies do not keep a stack at the top, so a newer stack can rise. The card still shows the all-time copy count.

Saved is the signed-in person's bookmarks. Only stacks that are still listed appear.

## Search and sort

Search runs in D1 with FTS5 over the stack name, the creator's display name, the goal tags, and the ingredient names and keys. A query that starts with "has", such as "has magnesium", searches ingredients only. Results are paginated.

Goal tags are sleep, energy, heart health, recovery, focus, and longevity. The sharer picks up to 3.

Sorts are newest, audit score, copies in the last 30 days, and outcome for people like you. That last sort uses only the viewer's age range and sex, and only stacks where at least 10 copiers in that same group have a 90 day window. Stacks under that bar are left out, and the count is not shown. Two different age or sex groups are never placed on one ranked list.

Prices are not stored on the share and are not returned by Discover.
