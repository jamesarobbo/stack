Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. Stack is an educational log. It is not a medical device and it does not calculate a dose for you.

What does Impact show?

For a chosen supplement start date, Impact compares deep sleep, HRV (SDNN), and resting heart rate across 21 days before and 21 days after, using wearable days from Apple Health. It is labeled as association only, not causation, and it does not tell you to start or stop anything.

What does the audit cover?

Overlapping ingredients across bottles, estimated monthly spend from the labels you enter, and a dose score from daily totals and overlaps.

Can I share a stack?

Yes. You can publish a shareable stack, copy one into your own list, and browse Discover with goal filters such as Longevity.
